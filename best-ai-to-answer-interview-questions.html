Why real-time matters more than raw intelligence

Many people assume the "smartest" model automatically makes the best interview assistant. In practice, response speed, audio accuracy, and a clean overlay matter far more than which large language model sits behind the tool. An interview moves fast. If you have to alt-tab, retype the question, and wait for a slow reply, you lose the natural rhythm of the conversation and it shows.

CloakAI is engineered around this constraint. It captures the question as it is asked, sends it through a fast response pipeline, and surfaces a concise, interview-appropriate answer within seconds, whether the question is behavioral, system design, or a live coding prompt.

How does cloaked AI stay invisible on screenshare?+
CloakAI uses Windows content protection, the same mechanism apps use to stop protected video being captured. It marks its own overlay as protected, so any screen capture, screen share, or recording simply does not include it. You see the answer on your screen; the person on the other end of the call sees nothing where the overlay is.
