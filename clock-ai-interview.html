CloakAI helps you structure answers using frameworks like STAR for behavioral questions, and walks through problem-solving approaches for technical rounds. During a live session, CloakAI listens to the question being asked and quietly generates a structured response outline you can glance at and build from. This is especially useful for candidates who freeze up under pressure or who want a safety net for unexpected curveball questions.

After each practice round, you can review what worked, which answers felt weak, and where you need more preparation. This turns every mock session into a measurable improvement cycle instead of a one-off exercise.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is cloaked AI?+
Cloaked AI is an AI assistant that stays hidden, or cloaked, from screen sharing and recording while it helps you in real time. In interviews, CloakAI is a cloaked AI: it shows answers on your screen during a live interview but is invisible to Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, and any screen recorder because it uses operating-system content protection on Windows.
Is Cloaked AI the same as CloakAI?+
People search the idea using many spellings, including cloaked ai, cloak ai, clock ai, cloak gpt, and cloaker. They are all referring to the same concept: an invisible AI assistant. CloakAI is the interview-focused product that delivers this, with real-time answers, resume personalisation, and OS-level screenshare invisibility.
How does cloaked AI stay invisible on screenshare?+
CloakAI uses Windows content protection, the same mechanism apps use to stop protected video being captured. It marks its own overlay as protected, so any screen capture, screen share, or recording simply does not include it. You see the answer on your screen; the person on the other end of the call sees nothing where the overlay is.
