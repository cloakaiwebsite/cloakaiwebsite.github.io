What Is Chiku AI Supposed to Do?

Chiku AI is marketed as an AI-powered interview support tool meant to help candidates during live interviews by suggesting answers or talking points in real time. Like most tools in this category, the pitch is simple: reduce anxiety, help you structure answers faster, and give you a safety net when a tough question catches you off guard.

On paper, that sounds useful for anyone preparing for coding rounds, HR screens, or panel interviews. In practice, the experience candidates report is mixed, and that mismatch between promise and performance is exactly why so many people search for reviews before committing.

Common Complaints in Chiku AI Reviews

When you dig through candidate discussions and review threads, a few patterns show up again and again:

  • Latency during live calls: Several users report noticeable lag between the question being asked and a usable suggestion appearing, which is a serious problem when you are on a timed technical round.
  • Generic answer suggestions: Responses can feel templated rather than tailored to the specific job description, company, or your own resume context.
  • Visibility concerns: Some setups are not as discreet as advertised, which creates stress rather than confidence during screen-shared interviews.
  • Limited coding support: For technical interviews involving live coding, feedback suggests the tool struggles to keep pace with complex, multi-part problems.
  • Pricing clarity: A number of reviewers mention confusion around what is included at each pricing tier versus what requires an upgrade mid-trial.

None of this means Chiku AI is useless for every candidate. But if you are preparing for a high-stakes interview where split-second reliability matters, these gaps are worth taking seriously before you commit your prep time and money.
