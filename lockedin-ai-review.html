What Is LockedIn AI?

LockedIn AI is a career platform that bundles a real-time interview copilot with a range of other tools such as coding help, an AI answer generator and resume features. The breadth is the selling point: it tries to be a one-stop shop for the whole job hunt rather than a single-purpose interview assistant. The live copilot listens to your interview and surfaces suggested answers as questions come up.

LockedIn AI Pricing and the Free Tier

LockedIn AI offers a free tier, which lets you try the basics without paying, and that is genuinely helpful for a first look. The catch is that meaningful real-time use sits behind the premium plans, which start around $49.99/month. As with most tools in this space, that is a recurring Western subscription billed in dollars, so the real cost for an Indian candidate is higher once you factor in card friction and renewal.

LockedIn AI Review: Strengths and Weaknesses

What it does well

  • Free tier. You can start without paying, which lowers the barrier to trying it.
  • Broad toolkit. If you want interview help, coding help and resume tools under one login, the bundle is convenient.
  • Real-time copilot. The live assistant works and keeps pace with a conversation.

Where it falls short

  • Browser-based visibility. Reviewers have noted the copilot can appear when you switch tabs or share specific windows, which undermines the whole point in a live interview.
  • Subscription cost. Premium from around $49.99/month is a recurring charge, not pay-per-use.
  • Jack of all trades. A wide toolkit can mean the live-interview experience is less refined than a tool built only for that job.
