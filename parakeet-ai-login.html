Parakeet AI Login: Access Steps and a Better Alternative for Interviews

CloakAI Team2026-11-179 min read

Looking for the Parakeet AI login page usually means one of two things: you already have an account and need to sign in, or you're evaluating Parakeet AI and want to see what logging in actually gets you before you commit. Either way, here's the quick answer: Parakeet AI is accessed through its official website using your registered email, password, or Google SSO. Below we walk through the login process, common access problems, and then show you exactly how CloakAI compares if you're choosing an interview assistant for an upcoming round.

How to Log Into Parakeet AI

Step-by-Step Login Process

  • Go to the official Parakeet AI website (search \"Parakeet AI\" directly to avoid phishing clones that copy login pages).
  • Click the sign-in button, usually top right of the homepage.
  • Enter the email you registered with, or choose \"Continue with Google\" if you signed up via SSO.
  • Enter your password and complete any two-factor prompt if enabled.
  • You'll land on the dashboard where your interview sessions, past transcripts, or subscription settings live.

Common Login Problems and Fixes

  • Forgot password: Use the \"Forgot password\" link on the sign-in screen; reset emails typically arrive within a few minutes, check spam if not.
  • Wrong sign-in method: If you originally signed up with Google SSO, entering a manual password will fail. Try the Google button instead.
  • Account locked after multiple attempts: Wait 15-30 minutes or use the password reset flow to regain access immediately.
  • Browser extension conflicts: Ad blockers or privacy extensions sometimes block login scripts. Try an incognito window.
  • Subscription expired: Some login issues are actually billing issues; check your card on file if you're mid-interview-prep and suddenly locked out.

What You Get After Logging In

Once inside, most AI interview assistants (Parakeet AI included) give you access to a dashboard for session history, a way to connect the tool to your video call, and account/billing settings. The real question candidates should ask isn't \"can I log in\" but \"does the tool actually help once I'm in the interview.\" That's where the comparison matters more than the login flow itself.
