Why Final Round AI Pricing Feels Confusing for Indian Users

Most international interview prep tools, including Final Round AI, price their plans in USD. For Indian job seekers, this creates a few recurring issues:

  • Monthly subscription costs translate to a much higher INR figure once you factor in exchange rates.
  • International card transaction fees (typically 2 to 3.5 percent) add extra cost on top of the subscription.
  • Billing cycles and refund policies are often written for US or EU markets, which can be confusing if you need to cancel after a single interview season.
  • Customer support timezones rarely align well with Indian working hours, which matters if you hit a billing or technical issue right before an interview.

None of this means the underlying product is bad. It simply means the pricing structure was not designed with the Indian job market in mind, where candidates are often preparing for multiple interview cycles (campus placements, lateral hiring, remote global roles) on a tighter budget.
