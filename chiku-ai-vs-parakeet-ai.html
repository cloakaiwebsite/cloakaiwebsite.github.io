Chiku AI vs Parakeet AI: A Practical Comparison for Interview Prep

CloakAI Team2026-09-208 min read

If you are researching interview prep tools, you have probably come across both Chiku AI and Parakeet AI. Both promise to help candidates feel more confident during technical and behavioral interviews. This guide breaks down what each tool offers, where they fall short, and why more candidates are switching to CloakAI for dependable, real-time interview support.

We are not here to bash either tool unfairly. Instead, we will look at practical factors: how invisible the assistant is on screen, how accurate the answers are, latency during live calls, and pricing. By the end, you will have a clear picture of which option actually helps you walk into an interview prepared.

What Chiku AI Offers

Chiku AI positions itself as a lightweight interview companion focused on quick prompts. It works reasonably well for simple behavioral questions but tends to struggle with multi-part coding problems or system design walkthroughs that require sustained context. Many users report the assistant losing track of the conversation thread after a few exchanges.

If you want a deeper look at Chiku AI's strengths and limitations, we have covered it separately in our Chiku AI review and alternative guide, which goes into specific use cases where it falls behind more robust tools.

What Parakeet AI Offers

Parakeet AI markets itself around speed and a clean interface. In practice, its response latency can spike during longer technical questions, which is risky when you are on a live call and need an answer within a few seconds. Some users also note that its coding support is limited compared to tools built specifically for technical interview practice.

Common Pain Points With Both Tools

  • Noticeable lag when questions get complex, breaking your natural flow on camera
  • Limited support for coding and system design compared to dedicated practice tools
  • Less polished screen presence, making discreet use during video calls harder
  • Fewer practice modes for mock interviews outside of live sessions
