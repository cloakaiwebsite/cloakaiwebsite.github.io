How does cloaked AI stay invisible on screenshare?+
CloakAI uses Windows content protection, the same mechanism apps use to stop protected video being captured. It marks its own overlay as protected, so any screen capture, screen share, or recording simply does not include it. You see the answer on your screen; the person on the other end of the call sees nothing where the overlay is.
